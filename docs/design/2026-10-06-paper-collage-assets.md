Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Original PNG generations remain under `C:/Users/user/.codex/generated_images/01a11027-6e30-7392-9c52-aaa74271fbfe/`. Project assets are encoded as WebP at quality 88 without changing composition or dimensions; six assets total approximately 2.3 MB.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production website illustration in a handmade paper-collage editorial style. Layered dusty-pink paper, vermilion-red card, pale sky-blue, muted mint-green, off-white. Imperfect scissor-cut edges, visible paper fibers, slight wrinkles, real physical overlaps and tiny natural shadows between paper layers. Rough thin black marker outlines deliberately slightly misregistered from the cut shapes. Tactile analog craft, playful sophisticated editorial composition, bold simple silhouettes, never glossy, digital vector, watercolor or photorealistic people. No text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no watermark. Keep objects wholly inside frame with 6% breathing room.
